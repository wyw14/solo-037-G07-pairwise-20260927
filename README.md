# WaveLab

WaveLab is a local HTTP service for tow-tank experiments. The original equipment and interlock pages expose a fixed commissioning diagnostic scenario. Operator measurements are a separate live data path.

Set `WAVELAB_DATA_DIR` to choose the archive directory (default `data/wavelab`). `POST /api/measured-runs` accepts `name`, `model_name`, `section`, and `carriage`. `POST /api/measured-runs/{id}/evidence` accepts `kind` (`wave_time`, `force`, `roll`, `pitch`, or `position`), `source_id`, finite `value`, and `recorded_at` in RFC3339 form. `GET /api/measured-runs`, `GET /api/measured-runs/{id}`, and `GET /api/measured-runs/{id}/analysis` return the supplied records and wave/force summary. Measurements are committed to `operator-measurements.json` before they are published in memory, and are reloaded when the service restarts. The archive does not automatically grant a safety permit or drive hardware.

Attitude sensors are registered per run with `POST /api/measured-runs/{id}/attitude-sensors` (`sensor_id`, `label`, optional `mount_location`, `expected_interval_ms`, `threshold_deg`); the registry is committed to `attitude-sensors.json`. Operators submit roll/pitch angle samples in degrees through the evidence endpoint above, and carriage positions as `position` evidence. `GET /api/measured-runs/{id}/attitude-analysis` reports, per sensor and axis, the extreme angle and any sustained abnormal intervals (|angle| above `threshold_deg` across at least two consecutive samples), each linked to the nearest carriage position, plus sensor outages (gaps between samples larger than `expected_interval_ms`) and sensors that never reported data. A `threshold_deg` of 0 disables abnormal-interval detection.

The `internal/probe`, `internal/force`, `internal/permit`, `internal/carriage`, `internal/track`, `internal/wave`, `internal/absorber`, `internal/attitude`, and `internal/journal` packages provide component logic for further work. Their diagnostic use does not imply that the original HTTP pages accept real sensor streams.
